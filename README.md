## Install

1. Open the download link above on your Android phone.
2. When the file has downloaded, tap it. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. Open **EKK Attendance** and log in with your **CPR number** and the **password** your supervisor gave you.
4. Follow the **Phone setup** steps until every step shows ✓ Done (location “Allow all the time”, notifications, battery).

If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**. The app is EKK's own app and is not on the Play Store.

## Privacy

Location is recorded only during your shift check-in / check-out windows and when you tap Overtime. You are never tracked all day.
