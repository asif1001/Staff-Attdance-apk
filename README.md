# EKK Attendance — staff app

## Android

**[⬇ Download EKK Attendance (APK)](https://github.com/asif1001/Staff-Attdance-apk/raw/main/ekk-attendance.apk)**

Version 1.1.0 · Android 7.0 or newer · 55 MB

### Install or update

1. Open the download link above on your Android phone.
2. When the file has downloaded, tap it. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager, then go back and tap **Install** (or **Update** if you already have the app — your login and records are kept).
3. Open **EKK Attendance** and sign in with your **CPR number** and the **password** your supervisor gave you.
4. Follow **App Permissions** until every step shows ✓ Done (location “Allow all the time”, notifications, battery, auto-start).

If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**. The app is EKK's own app and is not on the Play Store.

## iPhone

iPhones use the same screens as a web page — nothing to install from here.

1. Ask your supervisor to turn on **iPhone check-in** for your account.
2. Open **https://wareopes1.vercel.app/checkin** in **Safari**.
3. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**, then open **WareOps** from the new icon.
4. Sign in with your CPR number and password. In **More → App Permissions** turn on reminders.

On iPhone you tap **Check In** and **Check Out** yourself (iPhones do not allow automatic recording).

## Privacy

Android records your location only during your shift check-in / check-out windows and when you tap Overtime. The iPhone page uses your location only at the moment you tap. You are never tracked all day.
